Rasterized to 2048 x 1024 and 1024 x 512 outline masks for the decorative globe.
The 768 px / 512 px static posters are rendered from the same local globe scene.

Both satellite maps are converted to WebP at 2048 x 1024 for desktop and
1024 x 512 for small/coarse-pointer screens. Assets are bundled locally;
visitors do not make requests to NASA, GitHub or a texture CDN.
